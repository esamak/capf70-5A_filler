Evaluation

Abbreviated: the check pilot decides which maneuvers are needed (CAPR 70-1 ¶7.3), so required tasks are flagged but don't block the PDF.

Ops quals endorsements evaluated

Check each endorsement being evaluated. Its task section opens on the Tasks tab, and your initials print in its box on the form, unless one of its tasks is graded U or the ride is unsatisfactory.

Q QualifiedQT Qualified with trainingU UnqualifiedV VerbalNP Not performed

Only the grades the form allows for each task are shown (the form prints a dash in the others). Tap a selected grade again to clear it.

Required tasks (italic on the form, per CAPS 72-5) on an annual evaluation need a grade before the PDF can be made, and NP doesn't count. Any other task you leave ungraded prints as NP, shown with a dashed NP button. Tasks outside the aircraft's class or the checked endorsements are set to NP for you.
